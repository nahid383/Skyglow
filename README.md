🌠 What is SkyGlow?

SkyGlow is an interactive educational concept that teaches children about space weather through storytelling, characters, illustrations, and real-world scenarios.

The project was created for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025 — Stellar Stories Challenge.

Instead of explaining solar flares, geomagnetic storms, and space weather using only technical information, SkyGlow tells the story from the perspective of a solar flare traveling from the Sun to Earth.

This allows children to discover how events happening 93 million miles away can affect people and technology on Earth.

🎨 The Story

Imagine a solar flare leaving the Sun...

       ☀️
     THE SUN
        │
        │
        ▼
   🔥 SOLAR FLARE
        │
        │
        ▼

        │

      🌍 EARTH
        │
   ┌────┼─────┐
   │    │     │
   ▼    ▼     ▼
  ✈️   🛰️    ⚡
Pilot Satellite Power Grid
   │    │     │
   └────┼─────┘
        ▼
   👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 PEOPLE

Through this journey, children see the cause-and-effect relationship between solar activity and life on Earth.

For example:

- 🚜 A farmer may experience GPS-related problems.
- ✈️ A pilot may experience communication issues.
- ⚡ Power-grid operators may receive warnings.
- 🛰️ Satellite systems can be affected by solar activity.

These scenarios are part of SkyGlow's educational storytelling approach.

🔬 Scientific Foundation

SkyGlow is based on research surrounding:

- ☀️ Solar activity
- 🔥 Solar flares
- 🌍 Space weather
- 🌈 Auroras
- 🛰️ Satellite systems
- ⚡ Geomagnetic storms

NASA resources, Helioviewer imagery, and educational materials were used as references for scientific accuracy.
